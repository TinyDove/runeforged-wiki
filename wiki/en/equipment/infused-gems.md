# Infused Gems

Infused Obsidian, Infused Amethyst, Infused Lapis Lazuli, and Infused Ruby adjust regular main affixes on weapons and armor.

## Crafting and Use

Each gem uses a shaped `3×3` recipe: place one Infusion Gem in the center and eight matching materials around it. Combine the result with equipment in a crafting grid to apply it.

| Gem | Outer material | Regular main-affix effect | Rare-or-higher limit |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Infused Obsidian | Obsidian | All values ×`0.85` | 6 |
| Infused Amethyst | Amethyst Shard | Top affix value ×`2` | 4 |
| Infused Lapis Lazuli | Lapis Lazuli | Maximum value of non-T0 affixes `+20%` | 4 |
| Infused Ruby | Redstone Dust | Flat damage and damage reduction ×`1.5`; compound damage increases only its flat part | 4 |

Eligible items are affixable Common or Magic weapons and armor. One item can use one gem. Magic equipment keeps a regular main-affix limit of 3; the limits in the table apply from Rare quality onward. Masterwork is calculated after the gem adjustment.

## Related Rules

- Regular main affixes added by Ascension or Alienation follow the gem's limit.
- Reforging removes the gem; repair preserves it.
- Gems and Weapon Transmutation are mutually exclusive. Applying a gem removes an existing Transmutation.
