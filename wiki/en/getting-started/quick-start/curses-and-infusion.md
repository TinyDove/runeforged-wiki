# Curses & Infusion

## Cursed Equipment

Cursed equipment cannot use ordinary forging currency, enchanting, or repair. Directly obtained Cursed equipment generally contains regular affixes, extra curse-area affixes, and one negative curse; Alien and Rune-Cursed equipment use their own structures.

A Sealed Gem turns Rare equipment into Cursed equipment. A Celestial Sealed Gem creates a stronger curse area on Rare equipment or reshapes the curse area and negative curse on Cursed equipment.

## Alienation

Alienation preserves the item itself and applies several random changes. It may add or replace regular affixes, change a Tier, reroll values, distort a highest-Tier affix, empower Infusion, or add an Alien-specific effect. A random change may also preserve the current affix state.

Whenever Add Regular Affixes is selected, it attempts to add a random `1–5` compatible affixes. The final count depends on the random result, item capacity, and available affix pool.

## Infusion

Non-Common equipment can accumulate Infusion Value from kills. At 100%, it receives a separate Infusion Affix; weapons normally receive T2 and armor T3.

Cursed and Alien equipment stops after its first Infusion Affix. Nature Rune-Cursed Weapons can repeat the cycle.
