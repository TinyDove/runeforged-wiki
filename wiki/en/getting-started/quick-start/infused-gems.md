# Infused Gems

Infused Gems adjust regular main affixes before an equipment build is completed. They apply to affixable Common or Magic weapons and armor, with one gem per item.

| Gem | Effect | Rare-or-higher limit |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Infused Obsidian | All regular main-affix values ×`0.85` | 6 |
| Infused Amethyst | Top regular main-affix value ×`2` | 4 |
| Infused Lapis Lazuli | Maximum value of non-T0 regular main affixes `+20%` | 4 |
| Infused Ruby | Flat damage and damage reduction ×`1.5`; compound damage increases only its flat part | 4 |

To craft one, place an Infusion Gem in the center of a `3×3` crafting grid and eight matching materials around it. Combine the result with equipment to apply it.
